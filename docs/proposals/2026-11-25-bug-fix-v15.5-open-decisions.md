# Proposal: bug-fix report lists only open decisions (skill v15.5)

**Status:** proposed 2026-11-25 (waits for the user's yes; nothing changed in the routine yet)
**Trigger:** the project coordinator, 2026-11-25 08:53 UTC: the open-items pages stored the PR #59 decision twice (`decision-bug-fix-pr-59` and `decision-bug-fix-pr-59-2`), because items 2 and 3 of the bug-fix report `review/bug-fix/2026-11-25.md` were already answered or no longer needed but still sat under "Decisions needed". The open-items owner checked its helper and says it works as specified; the fix belongs in the report.

## Why
The report template of the bug-fix skill (step 9) says only "each held draft first ...; then the rest" for "Decisions needed". Runs carried answered items there with a note ("Answered on 2026-11-24 (card)", "No longer needed: #59 merged"), and every reader of that section (the open-items helper, the mirror `docs/audits/bug-fix.md` of step 8b, which copies the section) counts them as open.

## What changes
One place: the step 9 report template of `.notes/invest-bug-fix/SKILL.md` (v15.4 to v15.5; +3 lines, -1 line). Nothing else in the skill, the routine's prompt v14, its schedule or its model changes.

| Part of the report | Before (v15.4) | After (v15.5) |
|---|---|---|
| `## Decisions needed` | every decision, held drafts first; answered ones could stay with a note | only decisions still open on the user, numbered from 1 in each report; a carried item names the report that first asked it |
| new `## Answered since the last report` | (none) | one line per decision that left the list: where the answer is recorded, or why it is no longer needed; or "none" |

```diff
 ## Decisions needed
-<each held draft first: link, question, options, recommendation; then the rest>
+<only decisions still open on the user: each held draft first (link, question, options, recommendation), then the rest, numbered from 1 in each report; an item carried over from an earlier report names the report that first asked it. A decision the user has answered (an `OUT/decisions.md` entry, a pick on a decision card or their words in this thread) or one that is no longer needed is not listed here>
+## Answered since the last report
+<each decision of the previous report's "Decisions needed" that left it: one line with where the answer is recorded (the `OUT/decisions.md` entry) or why it is no longer needed; or "none">
 ## Assumptions
```
Removed: the old one-line template text of "Decisions needed" (replaced, its "held drafts first" order kept).
