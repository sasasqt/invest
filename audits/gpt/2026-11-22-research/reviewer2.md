## Conditions that would change the drop decisions

- Carry from supplied inputs is identifiable when the input adds valid contemporaneously published curve data, a verified same-formula PIT score, or contemporaneous reliable spot and matching one-month future. If the claim is that existing front-only history identifies the score, no common front-only observation resolves the counterexamples without a new restriction or input.
- A documented current PIT cash-dividend ledger or simultaneously valid unadjusted and split-only price data plus explicit corporate-action conventions could keep an equity-dividend hypothesis. Forward dividends implicit in equity index futures would support the paper's equity specification. No dividend input identifies commodity carry by Eq. (9).
- A replication label would require the original sample, point-in-time contract and LME conventions, matched dated GSCI excess-return series, month-end weights, turnover/cost rule and agreement with published results within predeclared tolerances. A separately validated local futures P&L and matched simple benchmark could support a transferable adaptation. A sufficient untouched sample and realistic net results could support an empirical claim.
- A matching contemporaneous ledger entry specifying the same commodity signal, outcome and timing would make this a continuation or duplicate. The inspected prior backlog records only a topic and already-owned methods.
