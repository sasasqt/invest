## Identifiability and accounting controls

Hold the entire front OHLCV history fixed, including a price of 100 at 15 days to maturity. Under a toy same-time linear maturity curve, extrapolate spot to day 0 and interpolate to day 30.

| Deferred price at day 45 | Synthetic spot | Day-30 price | Carry |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| 90 | 105 | 95 | +10/95 ≈ +10.5263% |
| 110 | 95 | 105 | −10/105 ≈ −9.5238% |
| 100 | 100 | 100 | 0% |

The first two rows produce opposite signs on identical observed front bars. The third is a flat-curve negative control. This resolves the identifiability question for the stated observation set and linear illustration. The exact input scope that would change it is a synchronous released curve or a trusted spot and matching maturity quote, or a separately verified PIT score.

For return accounting, hold two actual contract prices flat at 100 and 110 through a roll. A splice of those price levels jumps 10%. The sum of the same-contract P&Ls before and after the roll is zero before costs. Independent review derived that second control. Both controls are arithmetic, and the original repository was never executed. A total-return-adjusted equity bar also lacks the documented cashflow/timing inputs for the trailing-dividend substitution that was considered and dropped.

## Falsifiable test design

The first feasible experiment is a deterministic **synthetic mechanics check**. Before approved execution, freeze the packet, expected artifacts and oracle outside product source. Quote records declare release time, contract/root, settlement time, maturity, first-notice and last-trade dates, multiplier, currency and price. Use two strictly ordered quotes bracketing day 30 from the same valid snapshot. Reject invalid, unavailable or stale pairs at the fixed decision cutoff. Abstain around denominators at or below a preregistered positive floor. This positive-price specification has an explicitly limited domain. It must expose excluded observations and their reason. The paired table above, reversed input order, later availability, missing bracket, equal maturities and negative price cases cover the score. The flat-price roll control, a signed one-tick P&L fixture, explicit two-leg costs and collateral booked once cover the returns. Check settled quantities, both legs and their cashflows, trade times, and artifact additions/removals. Failure of any invariant falsifies the proposed mechanics. Exact precomputed expected outcomes go in the test packet.

A later historical adaptation would use monthly rank decisions at a fixed post-settlement cutoff and the next eligible open, with a fixed instrument set, exchange calendars and exposure normalization. A gross-one long/short carry portfolio can be compared with a passive same-universe baseline and a lagged contract-consistent momentum portfolio matched on exposure and eligible dates. A date-preserving score placebo and flat or lagged curves test timing and spurious effects. Use the same approved observations, return accounting and cost convention for all arms. Costs cover both roll legs, commission and spread/impact, with liquidity restrictions set before any outcomes are seen. Pre-register the cost/delay variants, tested signal/holding rules, development and untouched confirmation windows, uncertainty method and a minimum economically meaningful net paired return before seeking run authorization. Treat the hypothesis as unsupported if the frozen improvement threshold or predeclared cost/delay robustness fails, or if dependence leaves insufficient power. Retain failed and aborted trial rows and all variant identities. Equity-index dividends, GSCI-close replication, and next-open local accounting are distinct research specifications.

The strongest counterargument is that carry ranks load on inventory, momentum, and shared sector/regime shocks, while contract-level turnover and illiquidity can erase a net spread. The cheapest adequate next check is the paired synthetic oracle, or a separately validated precomputed score/return comparison if available within policy. Do nothing beyond the queue if credible PIT sources and primary accounting checks cannot be obtained.
